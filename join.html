Curious minds wanted.

I am actively recruiting Ph.D. students to join my research group in Industrial Engineering at the University of Nevada, Reno.

Who I am looking for

Two things matter most to me:

  • Curiosity. You keep asking why something works, and you enjoy chasing the answer.
  • Mathematical instincts. You like turning a messy problem into a precise model, and you can sense when an idea is right before the proof is done.

Relevant backgrounds include mathematics, statistics, computer science, industrial engineering, and related engineering disciplines. Students should be fluent in using AI tools responsibly while remaining able to independently validate generated reasoning, technical content, and code.

Potential research directions

Research may draw on adaptive learning, information theory, efficient kernel approximation, distribution estimation, and optimization. These methods can support applications in manufacturing, battery management, healthcare, quantum computing, and other complex engineering systems.

How to express interest

Please send the following materials by email:

  1. Your current CV.
  2. Your transcripts.
  3. A sample publication, if available.
  4. Three sentences explaining why you want to do a Ph.D. in my group.

Use the subject line Prospective Ph.D. Student — 2027 so I can identify your message easily.
